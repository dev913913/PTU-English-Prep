## Listen to *The Last Leaf*

**Author:** O. Henry  
**Source:** VOA Learning English — American Stories  
**Duration:** Approximately 14 minutes

### Before You Listen

Have you ever lost hope during a difficult time? Can another person's kindness give you the courage to keep going?

In this story, two young artists, Sue and Johnsy, live together in New York. When Johnsy becomes seriously ill, she begins to believe that her life will end when the last leaf falls from a vine outside her window.

Listen carefully to discover what happens and how an unexpected act of kindness changes the story.
