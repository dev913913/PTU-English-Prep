# Writing Skills — Unit IV

## Theory Notes | Preparation for PTU Exams

Unit IV develops the practical writing formats tested in PTU English examinations. Good writing is organised, audience-aware, accurate, and easy to scan. Learn the format first, then practise adapting it to the question.

### What this unit prepares you for

- Documenting information in a clear and useful format
- Writing formal reports with findings and recommendations
- Making concise notes from a passage
- Writing formal and informal letters for different purposes

### A simple exam method

1. Identify the audience, purpose, and required format.
2. Make a quick outline before writing the full answer.
3. Use headings, paragraphs, bullets, or numbering where they improve clarity.
4. Keep the tone appropriate: personal for a friend, formal for an authority or organisation.
5. Leave time to check format, spelling, grammar, and completeness.

> **Exam Focus:** PTU questions commonly ask for letters, reports, notes, and practical documents. Marks are awarded for both the quality of the ideas and the correct format.
