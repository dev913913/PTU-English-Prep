# Report Writing

## Meaning

A report is a formal, organised account of an event, survey, investigation, problem, or project. It presents information objectively and may end with conclusions and recommendations.

## Standard report format

1. **Title** — states the subject clearly.
2. **To/from/date or reporter details** — identifies the context when required.
3. **Introduction** — explains the purpose and scope.
4. **Method or background** — describes how information was collected.
5. **Findings** — presents the important facts and observations.
6. **Conclusion** — explains what the findings show.
7. **Recommendations** — suggests practical next steps.
8. **Name and designation** — identifies the writer.

## Style

Use a formal and objective tone. Separate facts from opinions, use headings, support findings with specific details, and avoid emotional or exaggerated language.

## PYQ-style practice task

You are a marketing manager. Your company plans to launch a new product. Prepare a report based on market research to assess its market potential.

### Model outline

**Title:** Market Research Report on the Proposed Product

**Introduction:** State the product, target customers, and purpose of the research.

**Method:** Mention surveys, interviews, competitor observation, or online research.

**Findings:** Discuss customer demand, price expectations, competing products, preferred features, and likely sales channels.

**Conclusion:** State whether the product has potential and under what conditions.

**Recommendations:** Suggest pricing, promotion, product improvements, and a limited trial launch.

## Worked example

### Market Research Report on a Reusable Steel Water Bottle

**Prepared by:** Marketing Manager  
**Date:** 20 August 2026

#### 1. Introduction

This report examines the market potential of a reusable steel water bottle for college students and office workers. The research considered customer needs, price expectations, competing products, and preferred buying channels.

#### 2. Method

Information was collected through a questionnaire completed by 120 students and employees, short interviews with five shopkeepers, and observation of competing products sold online and in local stores.

#### 3. Findings

- Most respondents wanted a leak-proof bottle that was easy to carry and clean.
- A majority preferred a price between Rs. 400 and Rs. 600.
- Customers considered durability, insulation, and a one-year replacement guarantee important.
- Online marketplaces and college-area shops were the most preferred buying channels.
- Existing competitors were cheaper, but few offered both insulation and a reliable guarantee.

#### 4. Conclusion

The product has reasonable market potential if it is durable, attractively designed, and priced within the expected range. Its guarantee and insulated design could distinguish it from cheaper alternatives.

#### 5. Recommendations

The company should launch a limited trial in two college towns, price the bottle at approximately Rs. 499, and promote it through student ambassadors and online demonstrations. Customer feedback should be collected after the first month before wider distribution.

**Prepared by:**  
Marketing Manager

### Why this works

The report does not merely say that the product is good. It explains how information was collected, separates findings from interpretation, and connects the recommendations to evidence. Specific details make a report sound researched rather than imagined.

## Report versus essay

An essay develops an argument in connected paragraphs. A report is easier to scan because it uses headings, factual findings, and action-oriented recommendations. Do not write a report as one long personal paragraph.
