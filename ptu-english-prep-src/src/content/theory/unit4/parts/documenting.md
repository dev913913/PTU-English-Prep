# Documenting

## Meaning

Documenting means recording and presenting information so that another person can understand, use, verify, or refer to it later. A document may be a notice, memo, minutes, application, agenda, record, statement, or set of instructions.

## Qualities of a good document

- **Accuracy:** facts, names, dates, and figures must be correct.
- **Clarity:** use simple language and avoid unnecessary ambiguity.
- **Completeness:** include the information the reader needs to act.
- **Order:** arrange information logically with headings or numbering.
- **Conciseness:** remove repetition and irrelevant detail.
- **Consistency:** use the same style, terms, and format throughout.

## Choosing a format

| Purpose | Suitable document |
| --- | --- |
| Announce information to a group | Notice or circular |
| Record a meeting | Minutes |
| Give short internal information | Memo |
| Ask for permission or action | Formal application or letter |
| Give instructions | Numbered procedure |

## Document checklist

Before submitting a document, check its title, date, intended reader, purpose, headings, supporting details, and closing or signature. A document should allow the reader to find the important information quickly.

### Practice task

Prepare a short notice for college students announcing a cleanliness drive. Include the date, time, venue, purpose, materials students should bring, and the name or designation of the issuing authority.

### Suggested structure

```text
NAME OF INSTITUTION
NOTICE
Date:
Title:
Purpose and essential details
Instructions or contact information
Signature
Name and designation
```

## Worked example: College cleanliness drive

**GOVERNMENT COLLEGE, AMRITSAR**  
**NOTICE**  
**12 August 2026**

### Campus Cleanliness Drive

The Eco Club is organising a campus cleanliness drive on **18 August 2026**, from **9:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m.**, starting at the main gate. Students will clean the gardens, collect plastic waste, and separate recyclable material. Participants should bring reusable gloves and a water bottle. All students are requested to register with their class representatives by 16 August.

**Riya Sharma**  
Secretary, Eco Club

### Why this works

- The title tells readers the subject immediately.
- The first sentence gives the organiser and purpose.
- Date, time, venue, tasks, materials, and deadline answer the reader's practical questions.
- The closing identifies the issuing authority.

## Other documents at a glance

### Memo example

**To:** All department representatives  
**From:** Student Welfare Officer  
**Date:** 12 August 2026  
**Subject:** Submission of volunteer names

Please submit the names of two student volunteers from each class for the cleanliness drive by 16 August. Send the list to the Student Welfare Office before 4:00 p.m.

### Minutes example

**Meeting:** Eco Club planning meeting  
**Date:** 12 August 2026  
**Decision:** The cleanliness drive will be held on 18 August at 9:00 a.m.  
**Action:** The secretary will arrange gloves and the class representatives will collect names.

The same information changes shape according to its purpose: a notice informs a group, a memo gives an internal instruction, and minutes preserve decisions for future reference.
