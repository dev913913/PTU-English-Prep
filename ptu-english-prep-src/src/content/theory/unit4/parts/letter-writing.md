# Letter Writing

## Types of letters

An **informal letter** is written to a friend or relative and uses a warm, personal tone. A **formal letter** is written to an authority, organisation, editor, employer, or other official reader and follows a fixed structure.

## Formal letter format

```text
Sender's address
Date
Receiver's name/designation and address
Subject
Salutation
Opening paragraph: purpose of the letter
Body paragraphs: details, reasons, or request
Closing paragraph: expected action or thanks
Yours faithfully/sincerely
Signature and name
```

## Informal letter format

Write the sender's address and date, use a personal salutation, introduce the subject naturally, develop the message in clear paragraphs, and close warmly with a suitable name.

## PYQ-style practice tasks

1. Write a letter to your friend discussing ways to keep the environment clean and healthy.
2. Write a letter to your friend about increasing road accidents and measures to reduce them.

### Planning guide

For the environment letter, include waste segregation, reduced plastic use, recycling, clean public spaces, trees, and community participation. For the road-safety letter, include speed control, helmets and seat belts, no distracted driving, traffic-law enforcement, safe roads, and public awareness.

> **Exam Tip:** Do not use the tone of a formal complaint in a friendly letter. The ideas can be serious while the greeting and closing remain personal.

## Worked example: Informal letter about road safety

24 Green Park  
Jalandhar  
12 August 2026

Dear Aman,

I hope you are well. I am writing because the increasing number of road accidents in our city has become a serious concern. Many accidents happen because people drive too fast, use mobile phones while driving, or ignore traffic signals. Some motorcyclists also travel without helmets, and many passengers do not wear seat belts.

We can reduce these accidents by following speed limits, wearing helmets and seat belts, and never using a phone while driving. The traffic police should enforce the rules strictly, improve street lighting, and mark crossings clearly. Schools and colleges can also organise road-safety campaigns so that young people learn responsible driving habits.

Please discuss this issue with your classmates. Small changes in our behaviour can protect many lives.

With love,

Your friend,  
Rohan

### Why this works

- The address and date are included, but no formal subject line is needed for a friendly letter.
- The opening introduces the reason naturally.
- The body explains causes before offering solutions.
- The closing remains warm even though the topic is serious.

## Worked example: Informal letter about a clean environment

24 Green Park  
Jalandhar  
12 August 2026

Dear Neha,

I hope everything is fine with you. Our neighbourhood has recently started a cleanliness campaign, and I thought I would share a few ideas with you. We should separate wet and dry waste, avoid single-use plastic, and send recyclable material to the collection centre. Keeping public spaces clean, planting trees, and carrying reusable bags can also make a real difference.

The campaign will succeed only when residents participate regularly. We can encourage people by placing labelled bins in common areas and organising a monthly clean-up drive. A clean environment protects health and makes the neighbourhood more pleasant for everyone.

Write back and tell me whether your college has started a similar campaign.

Yours lovingly,

Priya

## Formal letter mini-example

If the same issue is sent to the Municipal Commissioner, the tone changes:

**Subject: Request for regular waste collection in Green Park**

Sir/Madam,

I wish to bring to your notice that waste is not collected regularly in Green Park. The problem has created an unhygienic environment and may cause illness. I request the Municipal Corporation to arrange daily collection and place separate bins for recyclable and organic waste.

Yours faithfully,  
A concerned resident

The facts are similar, but the formal letter uses an official subject, respectful request, and formal closing.
