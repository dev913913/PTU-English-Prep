# Reading and Understanding — Unit III

## Theory Notes | Preparation for PTU Exams

Unit III develops the skills needed to understand an unseen passage and express its meaning in your own words. Work through the parts in order: read actively, locate the central idea, write concise answers, and check that your language is accurate.

### What this unit prepares you for

- Close reading and comprehension questions
- Short and four-sentence summaries
- Paraphrasing passages without changing their meaning
- Analysis and interpretation of literary and knowledge-based passages
- Translation between Hindi/Punjabi and English

### A simple exam method

1. Read the passage once for the general idea.
2. Read it again and underline keywords, facts, examples, and contrast words.
3. Identify the main idea before answering individual questions.
4. Answer in complete sentences using your own words where possible.
5. Keep the answer within the required length and check spelling, tense, and punctuation.

> **Exam Focus:** The supplied PYQs repeatedly test comprehension, paraphrasing, summarising, word meanings, and interpretation. The practice passages in these sections follow that pattern.
