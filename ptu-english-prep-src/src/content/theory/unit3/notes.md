# Reading and Understanding — Unit III

## Theory Notes | Preparation for PTU Exams

Unit III teaches you to do three things well: **understand** an unseen passage, **say it again** in your own words, and **write it correctly**. Work through the parts in order. Read each explanation, try the practice task *before* you look at the model answer, then take the part quiz.

### How this unit is organised

| Part | Skill | What you will practise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Close Reading and Comprehension | Understanding a passage | Answering who, why and how questions, word meanings, inference |
| 2. Reading Practice with Marking | Writing your own answers | Typing answers and getting marks, feedback and a language check |
| 3. Summary Writing | Reducing a passage | Four-sentence and one-third summaries |
| 4. Paraphrasing | Rewriting a passage | Same meaning in new words and structures |
| 5. Analysis and Interpretation | Looking beneath the surface | Theme, tone, evidence and implied meaning |
| 6. Translation Practice | Moving between languages | Hindi/Punjabi to English and English to Hindi/Punjabi |

### A simple exam method

1. **Preview.** Read the title and the first and last sentences to guess what the passage is about.
2. **Read.** Read the whole passage once without stopping, for the general idea.
3. **Mark.** Read again and underline names, dates, reasons, examples and contrast words such as *but*, *however* and *although*.
4. **Answer.** Find the lines that answer each question and write in complete sentences, in your own words where possible.
5. **Check.** Look at the length asked for, then check spelling, tense, articles and punctuation.

### Common mistakes in this unit

- Copying whole sentences from the passage instead of answering the question.
- Adding your own opinion when the question asks only what the writer says.
- Writing an answer that is too long, or too short for the marks given.
- Changing the meaning while paraphrasing or translating.
- Forgetting to check tense and spelling at the end.

### Before you submit: a 30-second checklist

- Did I answer exactly what was asked?
- Is every point in my answer from the passage?
- Did I stay within the word or sentence limit?
- Are my tenses consistent, and is my spelling correct?

> **Exam Focus:** The supplied PYQs repeatedly test comprehension, paraphrasing, summarising, word meanings and interpretation. The practice passages in these parts follow that pattern. Always follow the instructions and the marks printed in your own question paper, because the length and marks asked for can change from paper to paper.
