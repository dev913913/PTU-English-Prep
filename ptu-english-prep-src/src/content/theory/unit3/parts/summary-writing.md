# Summary Writing

## Meaning

A summary is a short version of a longer passage. It presents the main idea and the most important supporting points in a clear order. A good summary is brief, complete, objective, and written in your own words.

## Steps for writing a summary

1. Read the passage twice and identify its central idea.
2. Remove examples, repeated ideas, detailed descriptions, quotations, and personal opinions.
3. Combine related points and arrange them logically.
4. Use concise sentences and suitable linking words.
5. Give a short title if one is required and revise the final length.

## PYQ-style practice passage

Big cities can be a difficult choice for living. Housing is expensive, and the size of large cities makes them difficult to control. City-dwellers often face an unusual way of life, crowded roads, rush-hour traffic, and streets full of people. Cities may also contain places where residents feel unsafe because crime is more common. For these reasons, some people believe that the countryside offers a more natural and secure way of life.

### Task

Summarise and paraphrase the passage in **four sentences** in answer to this question:

**Why do some people refuse to live in big cities?**

### Model summary

Some people avoid big cities because housing is expensive and urban areas are difficult to manage. City life often includes traffic congestion, overcrowded streets, and a stressful routine. Crime can also make certain parts of a city feel unsafe. They therefore prefer the countryside because it may offer a calmer and more natural life.

## Common mistakes

- Copying every sentence from the original passage
- Adding an idea that the writer did not mention
- Making the summary longer than the original answer requirement
- Writing disconnected points instead of a unified paragraph
