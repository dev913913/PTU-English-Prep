# Summary Writing

## Meaning

A summary is a short version of a longer passage. It gives the **main idea** and the **most important supporting points**, in a clear order. A good summary is brief, complete, objective and written in your own words.

## Summary, paraphrase and précis: what is the difference?

| | Summary | Paraphrase |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Length** | Much shorter than the original | About the same length as the original |
| **What it keeps** | Only the main idea and key points | Every idea, in new words |
| **What it leaves out** | Examples, repetition, detail | Nothing important |

A **précis** is a formal summary with a fixed length, often about one-third of the original. Always follow the length stated in your question paper. The one-third rule is only a general guide.

## Steps for writing a summary

1. Read the passage twice and find the central idea.
2. Underline the key points. Cross out examples, repeated ideas, long descriptions, quotations and opinions.
3. Combine related points and arrange them in logical order.
4. Write in your own words, using short sentences and linking words such as *because*, *therefore*, *also* and *however*.
5. Check the length, then read your summary once more for meaning, spelling and tense.

## The four-sentence formula

When you are asked for a summary in four sentences, this plan works well.

| Sentence | Job |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | State the main idea |
| 2 | Give the first key supporting point |
| 3 | Give the second key supporting point |
| 4 | Give the conclusion or result |

## Worked example

**Passage:**

> Big cities can be a difficult choice for living. Housing is expensive, and the size of large cities makes them difficult to control. City-dwellers often face an unusual way of life, crowded roads, rush-hour traffic, and streets full of people. Cities may also contain places where residents feel unsafe because crime is more common. For these reasons, some people believe that the countryside offers a more natural and secure way of life.

**Task:** Summarise and paraphrase the passage in **four sentences** in answer to this question: *Why do some people refuse to live in big cities?*

**How to plan it:**

| Sentence | Idea taken from the passage |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Housing is expensive and big cities are hard to manage |
| 2 | Traffic and crowds make daily life stressful |
| 3 | Crime makes some places feel unsafe |
| 4 | So people prefer the calm, natural countryside |

Some people avoid big cities because housing is expensive and urban areas are difficult to manage. City life often includes traffic congestion, overcrowded streets and a stressful routine. Crime can also make certain parts of a city feel unsafe. They therefore prefer the countryside because it may offer a calmer and more natural life.

## Practice passage: plastic bags

> Plastic bags are cheap, light and convenient, which is why shops give them away by the million. But their convenience hides a serious cost. A plastic bag may be used for only a few minutes, yet it can remain in the soil or water for hundreds of years. Discarded bags block drains, cause flooding in towns, and are swallowed by cows and fish that mistake them for food. Several cities have therefore banned thin plastic bags or charged for them, and shoppers who carry cloth bags have helped to reduce the problem. Small habits, repeated by many people, can protect the environment.

### Task

Write a summary of this passage in **three sentences**. Give it a short title.

<details>
<summary><strong>Show the model summary</strong></summary>

**Title:** The Hidden Cost of Plastic Bags

Plastic bags are convenient, but they last for hundreds of years and harm towns and animals by blocking drains and being swallowed as food. Because of this, some cities have banned or charged for them. Using cloth bags shows how small habits of many people can protect the environment.

</details>

### A weak summary, and why

> "Plastic bags are cheap, light and convenient, which is why shops give them away by the million. A plastic bag may be used for only a few minutes. Cows and fish swallow them. Cloth bags are good."

This is weak because it copies phrases from the passage, includes a minor detail (shops giving bags away), and its sentences are not connected. It never states the main idea, which is that a convenient product causes lasting harm and that small habits can reduce it.

## Checklist for a good summary

- Does it state the main idea in the first sentence?
- Does it include only the key points, with no examples or opinions?
- Is it in my own words, and is each sentence connected to the next?
- Is it within the length asked for?

## Common mistakes

- Copying sentences from the original passage
- Adding an idea that the writer did not mention
- Making the summary longer than the question allows
- Writing disconnected points instead of one unified paragraph
- Giving your own opinion on the topic

> **Exam Tip:** If the question says "four sentences" or "in about 40 words", count them. A correct summary that is too long can lose marks.
