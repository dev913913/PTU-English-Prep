# Paraphrasing

## Meaning

To paraphrase is to express the meaning of a passage in different words and a different sentence structure. A paraphrase may be shorter than the original or about the same length, but it must **not change** the writer's message, facts, tone or the logical links between ideas.

Paraphrasing is different from summarising. A summary keeps only the main points. A paraphrase keeps **all** the ideas and changes only the wording.

## Five-step technique

1. Understand the complete passage before changing any words.
2. Replace suitable words with accurate synonyms, not random ones.
3. Change the sentence structure, voice or order where the meaning stays clear.
4. Keep technical terms, names and essential facts unchanged.
5. Compare your version with the original to check that no idea has been added or removed.

## Four ways to change a sentence

Use one or more of these together.

| Method | Original | Paraphrase |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Synonyms** | Regular exercise improves health. | Frequent physical activity benefits well-being. |
| **Active to passive** | The committee approved the plan. | The plan was approved by the committee. |
| **Change the structure** | Because it rained, the match was cancelled. | The match was cancelled owing to the rain. |
| **Change the part of speech** | He decided quickly. | His decision was quick. |

### Worked sentence

**Original:** The rapid growth of cities has put heavy pressure on housing and transport.

1. *Synonyms:* rapid = fast, heavy pressure = great strain.
2. *Structure:* make the cities the subject of an active verb.

**Paraphrase:** As cities grow quickly, they place a great strain on homes and transport.

The meaning is the same, but the words and the structure are new.

## Useful transformations

| Original form | Possible transformation |
| --- | --- |
| because | since, as, owing to, due to the fact that |
| although | even though, in spite of the fact that |
| people have adopted | people now follow |
| reduces waste | leads to less waste |
| is important | plays an important role |
| a large number of | many |
| in order to | to, so as to |
| as a result | therefore, consequently |

## Practice passage 1: Sustainable living

In recent years, individuals and communities have increasingly adopted sustainable ways of living to reduce their environmental footprint. This change includes recycling, composting, conserving energy, and choosing eco-friendly transport. People are also showing greater interest in renewable sources such as solar and wind power, local food production, and reducing waste. Sustainable living benefits the environment and improves public health and economic resilience by using resources efficiently and reducing dependence on finite supplies.

## Practice passage 2: The value of sleep

Many students treat sleep as wasted time and sacrifice it to study late into the night. However, scientists have found that sleep is when the brain sorts and stores what it has learned during the day. A student who sleeps well can remember facts more easily and concentrate for longer. On the other hand, a student who is always short of sleep becomes irritable and makes more mistakes. Good sleep is therefore not a luxury but a necessary part of successful learning.

**Task:** Paraphrase the passage in your own words, keeping every idea.

<details>
<summary><strong>Show the model paraphrase</strong></summary>

A lot of students think of sleep as lost time and give it up so that they can study late at night. Researchers, however, say that the brain uses sleep to arrange and save the information it has gathered during the day. Students who rest properly recall facts better and can focus for a longer period. Those who regularly lack sleep, in contrast, grow bad-tempered and are more likely to make errors. Healthy sleep is thus essential to learning and should not be seen as an extra.

</details>

## A wrong paraphrase and a right one

**Original:** Good sleep is not a luxury but a necessary part of successful learning.

| | Paraphrase | Problem or strength |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Wrong** | Nice rest is not an extravagance but a required piece of victorious studying. | Random synonyms. The sentence sounds unnatural. |
| **Wrong** | Students need sleep because they get tired. | Changes the meaning. The writer talks about learning, not tiredness. |
| **Right** | Sleeping well is essential for learning and should not be treated as an extra. | Natural wording, same meaning. |

## Checklist

- Is every idea of the original still present, and nothing new added?
- Have I changed both the words and the structure?
- Does it sound natural when I read it aloud?
- Are names, numbers and technical terms unchanged?

> **Warning:** A paraphrase is not a word-by-word substitution. If the grammar becomes unnatural or the meaning changes, rewrite the whole sentence.
