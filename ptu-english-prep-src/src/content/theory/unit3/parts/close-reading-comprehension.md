# Close Reading and Comprehension

## What is close reading?

Close reading means examining a passage carefully rather than reading only for its general topic. Notice the writer's main idea, supporting details, sequence of ideas, tone, facts, examples, and important words.

## A reliable method

1. Read the title or opening sentence and predict the subject.
2. Read the whole passage without stopping to write.
3. On the second reading, mark names, dates, reasons, results, comparisons, and contrast words such as **but**, **however**, and **although**.
4. Match each question to the sentence or idea that answers it.
5. Write a direct answer. Do not copy a long paragraph when one or two precise sentences are enough.

## Practice passage: Leadership

> A leader is made, not born. A true leader is not necessarily the person at the helm of affairs; a true leader is one whose position others would want to emulate. Such a leader guides others either by personal example or by directing a specific course of action. A sympathetic or democratic leader understands the problems of people at the workplace and encourages teammates and colleagues. In today's competitive world, advancement depends less on the number of years served and more on actual performance and results. Outstanding work raises a person's stature in an organisation and inspires others to follow. With firm determination and effort in the right direction, every individual can aspire to become a leader.

### Questions

1. Who is a true leader?
2. Why should a leader be sympathetic and understanding?
3. How can a person become a leader?
4. What is the role of outstanding performance in an organisation?
5. How can success be achieved?

### Answer check

- A true leader guides others and becomes a person whom they wish to emulate.
- Understanding other people's problems helps a leader support and encourage the team.
- Leadership can be developed through determined effort, good direction, and personal example.
- Outstanding performance improves an employee's position and inspires others.
- Success requires firm determination and sustained effort.

> **Remember:** A comprehension answer should come from the passage. Do not add unrelated personal opinions unless the question asks for them.
